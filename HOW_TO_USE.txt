PERSONAL DELIVERY INVESTMENT AND PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT PLATFORM v1.6
============================================================

OPEN THE APPLICATION
Double-click Open_Wealth_Intelligence_Suite.bat in this folder.
The suite opens locally in your default web browser. Keep this project folder
in place so the launcher can find 01_Source\wealth-suite\index.html.

RECOMMENDED DAILY WORKFLOW
1. Open Overview for the current factual snapshot.
2. Record dated Portfolio BUY and SELL transactions when they occur.
3. Review Watchlist and Delivery candidates.
   In Delivery, import an offline OHLCV CSV when you want Technical Trend.
   Use Delivery Paper Trading to test a selection for 1–3 months before
   deciding whether to use real money.
4. Review company history and ratios in Fundamentals.
5. Click Save, then export a backup regularly.

CORE WORKFLOW
Overview -> Portfolio -> Watchlist -> Delivery Screener -> Fundamentals

ACTIVE MODULES (5)
Overview      Read-only daily summary of existing suite data.
Portfolio     Holdings, BUY/SELL ledger, weighted cost, and realised/unrealised gain.
Watchlist     Companies to watch, study, buy soon, or reject.
Delivery      Existing ranked decision-support scores and ratings.
Fundamentals  Financial history, ratios, quality context, and red flags.

MACRO COMPATIBILITY
Legacy Macro data remains preserved in backups but is no longer part of the
active workflow. Its source and regression history remain in the repository.

RESEARCH LIBRARY COMPATIBILITY
Legacy Research Library data remains preserved in backups but is no longer part
of the active workflow. Opening or saving the platform does not rewrite it.

INTRADAY SEPARATION
Intraday trading is being developed as a separate satellite project. Existing
legacy Intraday data remains preserved in backups. The Wealth Suite does not
display or rewrite legacy Intraday trades or their two legacy settings.

STORAGE AND BACKUPS
The suite stores data in IndexedDB inside the local browser profile used to
open it. Data is not uploaded by the application. Different browsers or
browser profiles have separate storage.

To back up: click Export backup and keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere
safe. Overview then shows the backup date and whether it is Current (up to 7
days old) or Due. Export before clearing browser data, resetting the browser
profile, moving computers, or reinstalling the browser.

To restore: click Import backup, select a previously exported JSON file, and
confirm the replacement. Import replaces the suite's current browser data,
so export the current state first if it may be needed later.

PRIVACY AND USE
Your portfolio and other stored data remain in the local browser unless you
copy or share an exported backup. Protect backup files because they may contain
private financial information, including preserved legacy records.

Scores, ratings, and rankings are decision support only. They are not
automatic investment advice or buy/sell instructions.

OFFLINE PRICE HISTORY
In Delivery, use Import Price History CSV. Select a local CSV containing:
Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume. Common capitalization and yfinance-style
CSV headers are accepted. A ticker such as TCS or TCS.NS can be entered, or
inferred from a ticker-based filename.

Use at least 250 valid trading rows when possible. Technical Trend requires
200 rows for a complete evaluation; 50-199 rows are Limited Technical Data,
and fewer than 50 rows are Not Evaluated. Imported rows stay in local
IndexedDB and are included in normal JSON backups.

Technical Trend is entry guidance only. Strong Trend is not an automatic buy
signal or investment advice.

DELIVERY PAPER TRADING
Delivery contains four sub-tabs: Screener, Paper Portfolio, Paper Transactions,
and Performance Review. Paper records are simulations and never enter the real
Portfolio transaction ledger or real wealth totals.

Before the first Paper Buy, open Paper Portfolio and set Starting Paper Capital,
the maximum allocation per stock, and the maximum number of open holdings. Zero
capital intentionally blocks Paper Buy. From Screener, choose Paper Buy on a
company, confirm the dated price and quantity, optionally enter charges, notes,
and a reason, then save. The BUY keeps a historical snapshot of the Delivery
scores, rating, Technical status, data date, and reason so later model changes
cannot rewrite what was known at entry.

For Paper Sell, open Paper Portfolio and choose Paper Sell on the holding. Enter
a valid non-future sale date, quantity, price, and optional charges or exit
notes. Partial and full sales preserve realised results and transaction history.
Delete is only for correcting a bad record; it is never treated as a sale.

Paper prices use the latest close from an offline imported OHLCV file and show
its date clearly. This is historical data, not a live quote. If no imported
price exists, enter a Manual paper price with a mandatory date. Never enter an
invented price.

After 1–3 months, use Performance Review for realised/unrealised results,
drawdown, holding periods, entry-rating groups, Technical-status groups, and
30/60/90-day observations where enough price history exists. Small samples are
descriptive only. Paper performance is model evaluation, not investment advice,
and does not guarantee future returns.
